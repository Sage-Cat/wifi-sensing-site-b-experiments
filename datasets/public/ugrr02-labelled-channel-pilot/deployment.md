# Abstract deployment

The labelled pilot retained the same five fixed relative roles as the action
table: near-end managed radio/controller (`zone-Z01`), mid-zone receiver and
collector (`zone-Z02`), boundary protected-service client (`zone-Z03`),
adjacent-zone managed radio (`zone-Z04`), and far-end receiver and collector
(`zone-Z05`).

The two receivers supplied distinct cross-zone sensing views. Placement was
fixed for this pilot but was not randomized or replicated across sites. Exact
geometry, photographs, equipment identities, and environmental details are
excluded.
